Healthy Plate

Why a Balanced Meal Matters

A balanced meal provides the body with essential nutrients such as carbohydrates, proteins, healthy fats, vitamins, and minerals. Eating a variety of foods helps maintain energy levels, supports growth and development, strengthens the immune system, and improves overall health. Following a balanced plate method can make healthy eating easier. Aim to fill half of your plate with fruits and vegetables, one quarter with whole grains, and one quarter with lean protein sources.

Smart Snack Choices

Healthy snacks can help maintain energy between meals and prevent overeating. Nutritious options include fresh fruit, vegetables with hummus, unsalted nuts, whole-grain crackers, and low-fat yoghurt. Choosing snacks that contain both protein and fiber can help you feel fuller for longer and provide sustained energy throughout the day.

Nutrition Tips for Students

Drink Enough Water

Staying hydrated is important for concentration, memory, and physical performance. Drinking water throughout the day helps regulate body temperature, transport nutrients, and support healthy digestion.

Eat Colourful Food

Fruits and vegetables of different colors provide different nutrients and antioxidants. Red foods may support heart health, orange foods often contain vitamin A, and green vegetables are rich in vitamins, minerals, and fiber.

Quick Nutrition Note

Nutrition experts recommend consuming a variety of foods from all major food groups each day. Small healthy changes, such as choosing water instead of sugary drinks, can have a positive impact on long-term health. Preparing meals at home allows better control over ingredients, portion sizes, and overall nutritional quality.
